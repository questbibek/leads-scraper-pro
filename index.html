Features

Everything you need
to generate leads

A powerful sidebar that lives inside Google Maps — no switching tabs, no complex setup.

📍

Multi-Location Scraping

Enter multiple cities separated by commas. The scraper runs through each location automatically and tags every result.

🔗

Social Media Links

Automatically extracts Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, and LinkedIn profiles from business listings.

📱

Phone Numbers

Multiple fallback detection methods ensure maximum phone number capture, even for tricky listings.

💾

Persistent Storage

Results auto-save every 500ms to Chrome storage. Close your browser, come back later — your data is still there.

⏸️

Pause & Resume

Need to use Google Maps mid-scrape? Hit pause. Resume exactly where you left off with zero data loss.

📊

One-Click CSV Export

Download all your leads as a perfectly formatted CSV. Auto-downloads on completion or export manually anytime.

How It Works

Up and running in 60 seconds

No accounts, no API keys, no configuration. Just install and scrape.

1

Install Extension

Download from GitHub and load unpacked in Chrome developer mode.

2

Open Google Maps

Navigate to google.com/maps — the scraper button appears on the right edge.

3

Enter Your Search

Type your search term and locations separated by commas.

4

Download CSV

Hit Start Scraping. CSV auto-downloads when complete.
